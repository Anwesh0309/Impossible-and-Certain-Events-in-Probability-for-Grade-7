# Story slide images

The 8 files in `public/assets/images/` (`slide1.png` … `slide8.png`, 1024×1024) are hand-built vector illustrations in the
module's cosmic style. To replace them with AI-generated art, generate images with these prompts and save them under the same
file names (no code change needed).

**Shared style:** cosmic purple/indigo starfield, glowing gold gradient title text at the top, cartoon 3D-style objects
(dice, marbles, coins, spinners), rounded glass caption banner at the bottom, vibrant kid-friendly educational infographic, square 1:1.

1. **slide1** — Title "CHANCE IS EVERYWHERE!". A die, a coin and a sun-and-rain-cloud in three glass cards; below them the words NEVER · MAYBE · ALWAYS as coloured pills. Caption: "Probability = how likely an event is to happen".
2. **slide2** — Title "OUTCOMES, EVENTS & SAMPLE SPACE". One die showing 4 (outcome), six dice faces 1–6 labelled sample space, the even faces 2, 4, 6 glowing gold (event), formula P(E) = 3 ÷ 6 = 1/2.
3. **slide3** — Title "IMPOSSIBLE EVENTS — PROBABILITY 0". Three cards with red ✕ badges: a die with a floating "7", a bag of only red marbles with a ghost blue marble, a red/blue spinner. A probability line with a marker at 0. Caption: "No favourable outcomes means P(E) = 0".
4. **slide4** — Title "CERTAIN EVENTS — PROBABILITY 1". Three cards with green ✓ badges: six glowing dice (all < 7), a bag of only red marbles, heads and tails coins. Probability line with the marker at 1. Caption: "Every outcome favourable means P(E) = 1".
5. **slide5** — Title "THE PROBABILITY LINE". A wide red→green gradient line with five ticks 0, ¼, ½, ¾, 1 (decimals and percentages underneath, words Impossible / Unlikely / Even chance / Likely / Certain) and small icons above each tick. Caption: "0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1".
6. **slide6** — Title "THE SAMPLE SPACE DECIDES!". Three marble bags: all red (P(red)=1, CERTAIN), five red + one blue (5/6, LIKELY), all blue (0, IMPOSSIBLE), with arrows between them. Caption: "Change the bag → change the probability!".
7. **slide7** — Title "OPPOSITE PARTNERS". A gold/cyan split bar (A / NOT A), two example cards: certain ↔ impossible (1 + 0 = 1) and 3/8 + 5/8 = 1. Caption: "P(A) + P(not A) = 1".
8. **slide8** — Title "STEP INTO THE SIMULATION LAB!". A friendly blue robot named ZARA in a glowing neon portal, orbited by a die, a marble bag, a prize wheel and coins; labels for the three stations. Caption: "Ready to test your chance-detective skills?".
